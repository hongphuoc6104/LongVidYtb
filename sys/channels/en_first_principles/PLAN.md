## 3. Luật Viết Kịch Bản & Công Thức Hook
### Nguyên tắc giọng kể
- Open with a widely accepted assumption that produces an absurd paradox.
- Break the premise down to bedrock physical or mathematical truths.
- Use visual logic trees and balance scale analogies rather than abstract math.
- End with a practical decision framework for daily evaluation.
### Công thức Hook 3 giây đầu (viết 3 bản A/B/C, chấm điểm bằng `script_hook_scorer.py`)
- `The Prisoner's Dilemma: why being purely selfish backfires in the long run.`
- `Survivorship bias: the WWII airplane bullet holes everyone misunderstood.`
- `Goodhart's Law: what happens when a measure becomes the target.`
- `Second-order thinking: how great strategists see three moves ahead.`
### Cấu trúc giữ chân TikTok Short (`short_9x16`)
1. Hook <= 3s: shocking claim or relatable pain + striking first frame
1. Open loop: promise the answer, delay it
1. Escalation beats every 2-5s (new visual, number or twist)
1. Payoff: answer the loop in one clear image
1. Closing line
- **Câu kết (CTA):** Which move would you play in this paradox? Comment your choice.
### Cấu trúc giữ chân YouTube Long (`long_16x9`)
1. Cold open 0-30s: strongest scene of the video, no channel intro
1. Stakes: why this matters to the viewer's own life
1. 3-5 chapters, each opens with a mini-hook and ends with a re-hook
1. Pattern interrupt every 20-30s (story, number, visual gag)
1. Synthesis + practical takeaway
1. Closing line pointing to the next video
- **Câu kết (CTA):** Master our next mental model in depth on First Principles Ink.
### Điều cấm kỵ (Avoid)
- Unproven conspiracy models
- Dismissive intellectual arrogance
- Dense mathematical notation without visual proof
