# Content & Script Strategy — Kindred Sketch (`en_kindred_sketch`)

## 1. Standalone Packaging & V4 Short Funnel
- **Title Contract:** `youtube_title <= 55` characters. Strictly NO numbered prefixes (`P1:`, `Part 2:`, `Ep 3:`) on Shorts.
- **Funnel Twin:** Shares verified primary Fact Vault research with sibling channel `thuong_cho_kheo` (`./vp clone-en` / bilingual adaptation).
- **Short->Long Policy:** Declare `parent_long_id` when linked; place channel bridge + 2-choice debate question inside final `15%` (`youtube_closing_question` / pinned creator comment). Never say *"like/share/subscribe"*.
- **NotebookLM 3-Vault Grounding:** Compile verified primary sources into `fact_vault.json` via `sys/tools/notebooklm_vault.py` before drafting narration (`fact_grounding_linter.py --strict`).

## 2. Proven Narrative Spine (`Cinema Therapy / The School of Life`)
- **Sentence Structure Formula:** Công thức đối thoại tâm lý mối quan hệ: (1) Bối cảnh căng thẳng gần gũi ('When someone you love...') -> (2) Phán đoán sai lầm trực giác ('we assume they don't care') -> (3) Đảo chiều tâm lý giật mình ('Instead, silence is a scream of fear') -> (4) Câu hỏi tu từ cốt lõi ('Why do we attack when we want affection?') -> (5) Cơ chế phòng vệ vô thức ('nervous system perceives rejection as danger') -> (6) Đóng sập cánh cửa giao tiếp ('slamming down walls') -> (7) Phủ định phương pháp sai ('Closeness doesn't return by battering the door') -> (8) Đích đến gắn kết an toàn ('when you build safety together').
- **Long (`long_16x9`) Spine:** Cold open 0-30s: strongest scene of the video, no channel intro -> Stakes: why this matters to the viewer's own life -> 3-5 chapters, each opens with a mini-hook and ends with a re-hook -> Pattern interrupt every 20-30s (story, number, visual gag) -> Synthesis + practical takeaway -> Closing line pointing to the next video
- **Short (`short_9x16`) Spine:** Hook <= 3s: shocking claim or relatable pain + striking first frame -> Open loop: promise the answer, delay it -> Escalation beats every 2-5s (new visual, number or twist) -> Payoff: answer the loop in one clear image -> Closing line
- **20s Verbatim Rhythm Benchmark:** [00:00-00:03.2s] 'Wait, what was it, a phone interview? Wow.' [pause 0.36s] -> [00:03.6-00:08.3s] 'Miranda Priestly is famous for being unpredictable.' [pause 0.28s] -> [00:08.6-00:11.8s] 'Okay Doug, how is it that you know who she is and I didn't?' [pause 0.21s] -> [00:12.0-00:15.5s] 'I'm actually a girl... Well, that would explain so much.' [pause 0.35s] -> [00:15.8-00:20.0s] 'Look, seriously, Miranda Priestly is a huge deal. A million girls would kill for that.'
