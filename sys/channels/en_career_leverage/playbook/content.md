## 2. Proven Narrative Spine (`Wall Street Journal / HBR`)
- **Sentence Structure Formula:** Công thức đàm phán đòn bẩy Wall Street Journal: (1) Mệnh đề tiên đề quyền lực ('In executive salary talks, leverage is silent') -> (2) Phá vỡ định kiến chăm chỉ ('and hard work rarely earns it') -> (3) Bản chất thị trường phi đối xứng ('comes from rare, irreplaceable market alternatives') -> (4) Câu hỏi tu từ bất công đãi ngộ ('Why do top performers still get underpaid?') -> (5) Cơ chế định giá chi phí thay thế ('companies pay for replacement cost, not effort') -> (6) Nắm giữ mắt xích trọng yếu ('When you control a critical bottleneck, you dictate terms') -> (7) Lá bài tẩy tối thượng ('The ultimate negotiating power is walking away') -> (8) Đích đến hành động chủ động ('Build your leverage before you sit down').
- **Long (`long_16x9`) Spine:** Cold open 0-30s: strongest scene of the video, no channel intro -> Stakes: why this matters to the viewer's own life -> 3-5 chapters, each opens with a mini-hook and ends with a re-hook -> Pattern interrupt every 20-30s (story, number, visual gag) -> Synthesis + practical takeaway -> Closing line pointing to the next video
- **Short (`short_9x16`) Spine:** Hook <= 3s: shocking claim or relatable pain + striking first frame -> Open loop: promise the answer, delay it -> Escalation beats every 2-5s (new visual, number or twist) -> Payoff: answer the loop in one clear image -> Closing line
