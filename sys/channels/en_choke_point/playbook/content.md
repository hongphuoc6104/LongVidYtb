# Content & Script Strategy — Choke Point Maps (`en_choke_point`)

## 1. Standalone Packaging & V4 Short Funnel
- **Title Contract:** `youtube_title <= 55` characters. Strictly NO numbered prefixes (`P1:`, `Part 2:`, `Ep 3:`) on Shorts.
- **Funnel Twin:** Shares verified primary Fact Vault research with sibling channel `ban_do_quyen_luc` (`./vp clone-en` / bilingual adaptation).
- **Short->Long Policy:** Declare `parent_long_id` when linked; place channel bridge + 2-choice debate question inside final `15%` (`youtube_closing_question` / pinned creator comment). Never say *"like/share/subscribe"*.
- **NotebookLM 3-Vault Grounding:** Compile verified primary sources into `fact_vault.json` via `sys/tools/notebooklm_vault.py` before drafting narration (`fact_grounding_linter.py --strict`).

## 2. Proven Narrative Spine (`RealLifeLore`)
- **Sentence Structure Formula:** Công thức 5 bước RealLifeLore: (1) Số liệu khổng lồ về đội tàu thương mại trên đại dương ('Fifty thousand vessels traverse open oceans...') -> (2) Quy mô container hàng hoá khổng lồ ('carrying six million containers right now...') -> (3) Nút thắt cổ chai địa lý ngặt nghèo ('Yet ninety percent must squeeze through narrow straits...') -> (4) Câu hỏi khủng hoảng chuỗi cung ứng toàn cầu ('What happens when one canal shuts down?') -> (5) Bản đồ động mạch thương mại và kết luận địa chính trị tối thượng ('a single blockage strangles trillions in trade... Whoever controls these choke points commands the world').
- **Long (`long_16x9`) Spine:** Cold open 0-30s: strongest scene of the video, no channel intro -> Stakes: why this matters to the viewer's own life -> 3-5 chapters, each opens with a mini-hook and ends with a re-hook -> Pattern interrupt every 20-30s (story, number, visual gag) -> Synthesis + practical takeaway -> Closing line pointing to the next video
- **Short (`short_9x16`) Spine:** Hook <= 3s: shocking claim or relatable pain + striking first frame -> Open loop: promise the answer, delay it -> Escalation beats every 2-5s (new visual, number or twist) -> Payoff: answer the loop in one clear image -> Closing line
- **20s Verbatim Rhythm Benchmark:** [00:00-00:05.0s] 'At any single time, there are around fifty thousand merchant vessels carrying as much as six million containers...' [pause 0.18s] -> [00:05.2-00:11.0s] '...full of cargo and goods all across the world's oceans and between all the world's continents.' [pause 0.99s] -> [00:12.0-00:20.0s] 'These networks and streams of ships that you see moving in lines across the world's surface here are the literal arteries that power the beating heart of the global economy.'
