# Pacing & Script Rules — Bản Đồ Quyền Lực (v3)

Reference contract for conversational Vietnamese geopolitical & resource-economics narration, 4-mode visual beat mapping (`Long 16:9`), and standalone `Short 9:16` funnel spin-offs.

## 1. Zero-Filler Geopolitical Narrative Architecture (`Long 16:9`)

Every `long_16x9` episode on **Bản Đồ Quyền Lực** targets **`~9m30s–10m00s`** (`240` visual beats, average **`~2.42s/image`**) across **`9–10` Chapters**:
- **Cold Open (0–30s, Chapter 1)**: Open directly in the middle of a physical supply-chain fracture, maritime bottleneck, or concrete price shock impacting the viewer's everyday life — zero channel intros ("Chào mừng bạn đến với...").
- **Zero-Filler Conversational Spoken Cadence**: Write short, clear Vietnamese spoken sentences calibrated for `VietNarrator_ban_do_quyen_luc` (`recommended_speed=1.15`, `default_tone="authoritative_clear"`, master `-11.0 LUFS / -1.0 dBTP`). Eliminate textbook jargon unless immediately anchored to a concrete physical image or everyday object (phone chip, container ship, refining vat, power cable).
- **Chapter Escalation & Micro-Hooks**: Every chapter opens with a geographical or resource riddle and closes with a forward pivot ("nhưng nắm được mỏ quặng mới chỉ là bước một...") that pulls viewers into the next chapter.

## 2. Beat-to-4-Mode Visual Mapping (`Long 16:9`)

Instead of leaving 240 consecutive images as uniform slides, assign each beat to one of the 4 storytelling modes in [visual-grammar-4styles.md](visual-grammar-4styles.md) based on its rhetorical function:

1. **Attach `CACH_1_HERO_STAMP` (~15% beats)** whenever the spoken line drops a concrete strategic percentage, dollar/tonnage figure, time shock, or 2-word concept pivot (`"92% TINH LUYỆN"`, `"17 NGUYÊN TỐ"`).
2. **Attach `CACH_2_SPLIT_COMPARE` (~12% beats)** whenever the sentence contrasts two countries, two strategies, monopoly vs dependency, or raw extraction vs high-value manufacturing.
3. **Attach `CACH_3_DOSSIER_LIST` (~15% beats)** whenever the narration unpacks a 3-point list, 3 refining barriers, or 3 geopolitical levers in sequence.
4. **Use `CACH_4_CINEMATIC_PACING` (~58% beats)** for storytelling momentum, physical movement, industrial scale, and atmospheric breathing room between graphic emphasis beats. Never stack two identical overlay layouts back-to-back unless intentionally stepping through items `[01] -> [02] -> [03]` of the same dossier card.

## 3. Standalone Short `9:16` Spin-Off Arc (`48–58s`, `12–14` Beats)

Standalone `short_9x16` episodes (`omni_flash` continuous I2V motion, `1440x2560@30fps`, linked to `parent_long_id`) follow a tight 4-act micro-arc:

1. **Beats 1–2 (`0–8s`) — Truth-Slap Geopolitical Hook**: Start immediately with a counter-intuitive consumer shock tied to a chokepoint (`<= 18` syllables on beat 1, zero dead air).
2. **Beats 3–6 (`8–24s`) — Physical Bottleneck Proof**: Show the exact physical reason behind the paradox (why mines aren't enough, why strait width matters, why refining pollutes/locks supply).
3. **Beats 7–10 (`24–42s`) — Consequence & Contrast**: Contrast monopoly leverage vs global vulnerability with sharp visual contrast.
4. **Beats 11–14 (`42–58s`, last 15%) — Close CTA & Channel Bridge**: Deliver a thought-provoking question/close CTA + natural bridge inviting viewers to watch the full episode on **Bản Đồ Quyền Lực** (never say "like/share/subscribe").
