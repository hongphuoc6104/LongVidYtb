# Full-frame authoring contracts

New images lock to the **2D Vietnamese Indie Doodle** profile `1.7.0` (`handdrawn_2d_explainer` — `Nguệch Ngoạc / Vẽ Vời / Xấu Vê Nờ` raw MS-Paint / Flash webcomic aesthetic) defined in `sys/visual_style.py` and `sys/flow_prompts/versions/1.7.0/registry.json`. Follow [visual style](../vp-production/references/visual-style.md): use uniform pitch-black (`#000000`) freehand marker contours with visible unpolished hand-drawn wobble, 100% unshaded flat MS-Paint solid hex fills (`#F8F0DC` paper-cream, `#F2B88B` apricot-peach, `#93A88C` sage-green, `#CBE0E0` pale ice-blue, `#2E4361` night slate-navy, `#FFFFFF` pure white), strict 2D front-on/side-on orthographic cutout staging, and zero floating oval drop shadows, zero 3/4 isometric perspective, zero sleek rounded AI vector icons, and zero dark renderer vignettes. Include people only when explicitly needed (`has_character: true`) using the iconic slightly lopsided pure-white (`#FFFFFF`) bald circle-head doodle stickman (`#3B82C4` flat cerulean-blue T-shirt, single-stroke black wire arms ending in round white circle hands, `#8C857B` flat gray trousers or black stick legs, tiny low-set black dot eyes leaving a large open upper cranium for `cutaway_detail` gags). Existing pinned jobs retain their original style.

- New full-frame jobs declare `visual_pacing_profile: full_frame_v1`. Older snapshots remain readable; do not edit saved revisions or journals to migrate them.
- Landscape defaults to external SRT (`subtitles: false`); portrait keeps burn-in subtitles and its renderer safe area. Full-frame image prompts reserve no caption band in either aspect. Keep backgrounds continuous to the canvas edges, and suppress dark edge vignettes on `handdrawn_2d_explainer`.
- Canonical `VietNarrator` uses `speed: 1.0` across both long videos (`long_16x9`) and short spin-offs (`short_9x16`). The contract permits `[0.90, 1.15]`; scene-level `tone` modulation (`0.98`, `1.02`, `0.92` via `resolve_tone_speed`) is validated against `allowed_speeds`. A speed multiplier is not a measured syllable rate.
- A sentence is a TTS boundary. Keep bilingual parts and gaps aligned with sentence chunks. SRT cues never append the opening words of the next sentence.
- Author distinct visual settings: setting descriptions are strictly limited to $\le 8$ words specifying only flat 2D color stage/space (strictly no 3D lighting cones, prop lists, or furniture enumeration). No more than two consecutive scenes in one setting; for ten or more scenes no setting exceeds 30%. Progress across the 4 functional cognitive phases (`narrative_phase`: `lived_friction` → `empirical_conflict` → `structural_model` → `actionable_resolution`) without requiring hardcoded school-room nouns.
- Maintain visual breathing room: at least 30% of beats in every 10-beat sliding window must be character-free rest frames (`still_life`, `environment`, `visual_diagram`, `cutaway_detail`, character-free `white_card`), and never exceed 4 consecutive beats containing `full_scene`/`variation` human figures.
- Use all 10 standard shot types: `full_scene`, `variation`, `macro_prop`, `visual_diagram`, `concept_metaphor`, `cutaway_detail`, `white_card`, `white_isolated`, `still_life` (character-free object/document focus), and `environment` (character-free wide establishing shot).
- Reserve roughly the final 15–20% for practical instructions. Do not repeat the same pair of operations in more than three scenes. Cite actual experiments; never invent retention percentages or brain localization to populate a visual zone.
- Within one authored setting, returning full-scene shots reuse the established base via `based_on`. Preserve room geometry, windows, furniture and props. Include an expression lock in `preserve`, or declare emotional shifts explicitly in `change` / set `allow_expression_shift: true`. Independent locations need independent compositions.
- When hands or people are explicitly requested (`has_character: true`), use the iconic white-head doodle stickman anatomy (`CAST_APPEARANCE` / `DEFAULT_OUTFIT`) with bold `#000000` wobbly marker contours, single-line black wire arms, and round white circle hands. Empty `allowed_text` means abstract wavy ink lines only on papers/books, without words, titles or equations; when `allowed_text` is declared in `1.7.0`, use concise uppercase Vietnamese labels ($\le 6$ words, $< 5\%$ frame height).
- `transition: cut` describes the edit; `camera_motion` describes movement inside the frame. Use 4-direction semantic zoom/pan/punch motions (`push_in`, `pan_right`, `pull_back`, `pan_left`) without consecutive duplicates (`entropy >= 1.5` bits). Diagram/white-card comparison beats may use intentional `hold`.
- The direction diversity above describes authored intent, not mandatory visible motion on every image. Calm playback in `renderer/camera.mjs` takes priority: hold independent shots shorter than 2.4s and all macro/diagram shots; map pans, slides and shake to steady framing; turn punch into a restrained push. Use at most 1.5% zoom with peak scale velocity <= 0.004/s (including overscan), and allow at least 4s of camera rest before another moving composition. Consecutive variations/reused images share one camera clock, focal origin and portrait framing mode. Preserve the outgoing shot's own camera during dissolves. Full-frame playback never enables line-boil displacement.
- Overscan `1.045` crops about 2.15% from each edge at rest. It provides a framing margin, not a guarantee that provider marks disappear. Inspect real edge pixels over the entire motion range before claiming removal.
